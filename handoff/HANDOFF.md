**Disclosure — how the films were made.** They are frame-stepped renders, not real-time screen recordings: Chromium (Playwright) at 1600×900 CSS px; each frame is rendered through CDP `Page.captureScreenshot` with a clip equal to the camera window (centre + zoom, in CSS px) at scale 1.2 × zoom, so every frame is 1920×1080 and zoomed frames are rasterised at full resolution rather than upscaled. The page clock is advanced exactly 1000/60 ms per captured frame (`page.clock.install`/`runFor`), so animation timing is deterministic and does not reflect measured GPU or network performance. Document loads are awaited in real time between frames (the frame clock pauses). The pointer is an SVG arrow drawn inside the page at the real mouse position; each click is a real mouse down/up on the real element. Editorial zooms are pointer-led and keyframed in the capture (1.7× on source-page highlights, 1.6× on the chart, the quarter card and the mechanism bars; quintic ease, zoom interpolated in log space; the camera window never leaves the page); each zoomed shot holds about 2 s (60 s cut) / 1.7 s (30 s cut) before the next move. Both cuts click Q1 2001 within about one second of the chart appearing, so the filed page and the Committee page are on screen while the two tracks separate. The 60 s opener ("stack of filings", 0–4.4 s) is `qa/film/stack.html`, a composition of seven real pages from the dataset (2000 10-K cover, Q1 2001 10-Q p. 4, June 25 2002 8-K p. 5, Committee report cover, Rakoff opinion p. 1, Ebbers indictment p. 1, 2002 10-K p. 130). Frames were captured from a local copy of the deployed commit served over HTTP, not from the hosted URL. Encoded with ffmpeg (libx264; delivery crf 20 yuv420p high@4.2, master crf 10 yuv444p). The project repo is at its 5 GiB cap (a few MiB free when this build was submitted), so the repo holds bitrate-fitted copies of the two cuts (`films/paper-trail-60s-repofit.mp4` ≈ 9.8 MB, `films/paper-trail-30s-repofit.mp4` ≈ 3.6 MB; same frames, two-pass libx264 at 1300 / 950 kb/s — reading holds are visually identical to the deliveries, fast camera moves are softer); the full-quality deliveries (crf 20: 26.9 MB and 12.3 MB) and the clean masters are shared as assets in the build conversation, with every SHA-256 below.

## Limitations and errata (also in the app's Method & limits panel)

- WorldCom never filed restated quarterly statements. The quarterly "corrected" line is our reconstruction (reported minus the Committee's quarterly line-cost entries); it deducts only line-cost items, not the revenue items or other restatement categories, and it is labelled as a reconstruction wherever it appears.
- Fourth quarters are derived (FY minus nine months) and absorb reclassifications between the Q3 10-Q and the 10-K; e.g. Q4 2001 pre-tax income derives to $397m against the $401m the Committee cites.
- Comparatives change between filings (Q1 2000 line costs $4,092m as filed vs $3,733m as a comparative a year later; Q1 2001 revenue $9,720m vs $8,825m after Embratel's deconsolidation). The ledger uses the figure as first filed. The 2002 10-K's "previously reported" annual figures (pre-tax 2,375 / 7,581) differ from the original filings (2,393 / 7,568) because of reclassifications; footnoted in the restatement panel.
- The SEC complaint's quarterly "actual" column equals the reconstruction except Q2 2001 ($610m in the complaint vs $560m in the Committee's entries), disclosed in the chart's SEC rows.
- Annual totals differ between authorities ($9.25bn / $7.683bn / ~$9bn) and are shown side by side rather than reconciled.
- Page conventions: EDGAR filings are paginated by their own `<PAGE>` markers, so app page numbers differ from printed folios (the viewer shows both, e.g. Committee report app p. 110 = printed p. 105); the Rakoff opinion PDF is OCR text with noise; uncited pages of long PDFs are not rendered as images.
- The DOJ's published copy of the Ebbers superseding indictment is undated; the register marks it "undated copy" and the timeline dates the nine-count indictment from the Second Circuit's opinion. One earlier Method sentence (a $27m Q4 2000 revenue difference) could not be verified and was removed (reviewer D5).
- The verification sample (44) and the reviewer's sample (10) are samples, not a full re-read of all 379 page-cited values; the anchor re-location check covers all 379 mechanically.
